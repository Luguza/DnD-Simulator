answered when the human reviews the finished product. An answer that overturns
a choice becomes ordinary work for the queue.

| 1 | `w1d` | By what distance is an area of effect measured? | 3 | `w1d` | A grid cone's width in squares is always odd, so the rule's even widths — 10 feet, 20 feet — have to round.
