# Questions for the human

Decisions the task loop took instead of stopping to ask
(`docs/workflow.md` § Questions for the human). Nothing waits on these; they are
answered when the human reviews the finished product. An answer that overturns
a choice becomes ordinary work for the queue.

| 1 | `w1d` | By what distance is an area of effect measured? Chebyshev makes Fireball's 20-foot radius a 9x9 block of squares rather than a disc. | **Chebyshev, the one metric this grid already uses.** A square four diagonal steps out is 20 feet away under the rule that a diagonal step costs 5 feet, so the template asks `distance_ft` like reach does. A second metric would make "20 feet" name one set of squares to an attack and another to a spell, which is the drift `in_reach` exists to prevent. | A Sphere would cover ~69 squares instead of 81, so every area spell would catch fewer targets and `w5a`'s spells and DESIGN.md § AoE's placement search would both move. It would also be the first place in the project where two distances disagree, so it needs `distance_ft` to grow a second mode rather than a template to grow a filter. |
| 3 | `w1d` | A grid cone's width in squares is always odd, so the rule's even widths — 10 feet, 20 feet — have to round. Up or down? | **Up**: `2 * lateral <= along`, three squares of width at 10 feet rather than one. Rounding down leaves a 10-foot cone one square wide for its whole length and a 20-foot cone three, narrower than the rule draws at every second step. | Every cone catches fewer squares: a 15-foot Burning Hands goes from 7 squares to 5. That moves each cone spell's target count in `w5a` and in DESIGN.md § AoE's placement search, and the change is one comparison in `cone()`. |
| 2 | `w1d` | Where is a creature's footprint anchored — the low corner of its square, or its centre? | **The low corner**, so `position` keeps meaning one square. A 2x2 and a 4x4 have no centre square, so centring is defined for Huge and for nothing else. | Only Large and Gargantuan would need a convention invented for them, and `Creature.position` would stop being a square a caller can name. Every placement in `builds/` and in the scenario spec would shift by a square. |
